Provide evidence that can inform licensing discussions, reporting and the development of new royalty compensation arrangements. Commercial terms and royalty allocation rules remain matters for the parties involved.

Gain new visibility into how repertoire contributes to generative AI music, across both publishing and master recording rights. Use influence measurements to inform the fair distribution of incoming royalties amongst upstream stakeholders.
