Music shapes AI. We measure how.

The next challenge in music licensing.

Generative AI models learn from music. But when those models create something new, determining which works influenced the result, and to what extent, remains a difficult problem.

As the industry moves toward licensed AI, that question becomes increasingly important. Agreements establish what music can be used. Attribution can help establish how that music contributes to what comes next.

Fold is developing the technology to help answer that question.

Looking inside the model.

LUMINA takes a model-based approach to training-data attribution. It examines signals within generative AI models to measure the influence of individual training works on generated music.

The goal is to give music rights owners and AI developers more useful evidence of influence, beyond what can be learned from listening to or comparing finished recordings.

Our research focuses on making those measurements reliable, reproducible and useful in commercial settings.
