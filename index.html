The Problem We Solve

AI trains on copyrighted music.
Nobody gets paid.

The Problem

Generative AI models use millions of copyrighted songs to train. Artists receive no credit and no compensation. Legal disputes are stacking up with no resolution.

What We Built

Every AI model carries Causal DNA, traces of what it learned from. LUMINA reads that DNA during generation and identifies which training data caused the output.

What Changes

Every AI-generated song triggers a royalty payment. Rights holders see exactly who contributed and how much. Revenue flows automatically.

The Product

LUMINA
Reads the Causal DNA inside AI.

Other tools compare how songs sound. LUMINA reads the AI model itself to prove which training data caused the output. We call this Causal DNA.

How It Works

Three steps. Infringement becomes revenue.

LUMINA sits inside AI music platforms. Every time a song is generated, rights holders get identified and paid.

1

Embed

LUMINA installs directly into Gen-AI platforms like Suno and Udio. It runs at the generation layer, in real time.

2

Attribute

For every generated song, LUMINA identifies which labels, publishers, and artists contributed to the output.

3

Pay

AI platforms pay licensing fees. Rights holders receive royalties automatically through a verifiable distributed ledger.
