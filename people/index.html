Eric Reithler-Barros

CEO · New York

30+ years at the crossroads of music, media, tech and entertainment.

Read bio of Eric Reithler-Barros

Eric Reithler-Barros, MBA, is a New York City media executive with a 30+ year career at the crossroads of music, media, tech and entertainment. Eric was founding partner and CCO/CSO of RCRDSHP, a successful music NFT platform financed by the buyers of the $69M Beeple digital artwork, Dapper Labs, and Roham Gharegozlou.

Prior, Eric was the MD and CCO of A2LiVE/STORM Festival, the largest touring electronic music festival in Asia. He was also the Global Partnerships VP of SFX Entertainment (NASDAQ:SFXE) in NYC, the $1.4B conglomerate that acquired 62 of the biggest brands in the EDM industry (Tomorrowland, Beatport, Stereosonic, Sensation, etc).

He has been disrupting the digital music space since his days as Head of BD at Lime Wire, the famed New York p2p network, in the early 2000s. He is also a creator, having founded 4 successful labels, with over 200 records of his own released on as many labels around the world in a 34-year DJ and record production career. He is currently a visiting professor at Berklee College of Music's master's program in global entertainment & music business.

RD

Rob Dickins C.B.E.

Former Chairman, Warner Music UK · London / NYC

Former Chairman of Warner Music UK, the BPI and UK Music.

Read bio of Rob Dickins C.B.E.

Rob Dickins, CBE, is one of the most influential executives in British music history. As former Chairman of Warner Music UK, he signed and nurtured a legendary roster that includes Madonna, Enya, Seal, Simply Red, The Smiths, R.E.M., and Cher, helping generate billions in global sales. He also served as Chairman of the British Phonographic Industry (BPI) and was a founding trustee of the BRIT Trust.

Following his tenure at Warner, he co-founded Instant Karma Records with Sony Music and later became Chairman of UK Music, the industry's umbrella body.

Rob is renowned for his strategic foresight, A&R instincts, and industry leadership across policy, rights, and digital transformation. He was awarded a CBE (Commander of the British Empire) for services to the music industry and continues to advise top-tier entertainment ventures and rights organizations worldwide.

SC

Scott Cohen

Co-founder, The Orchard

Co-founded The Orchard; first Chief Innovation Officer at Warner Music Group.

Read bio of Scott Cohen

Scott Cohen is one of the music industry's most influential digital pioneers and innovators, with a career spanning more than 25 years. As co-founder of The Orchard in 1997, widely recognized as the world's first digital music distribution company, Cohen reshaped how independent artists and labels reach global audiences. The Orchard is now owned by Sony Music and generates more than $2 billion in annual revenue.

In 2019, he joined Warner Music Group as their first-ever Chief Innovation Officer, focusing on emerging technologies and the future of the recorded music business. He later founded JKBX (Jukebox), a platform designed to convert income streams from hit songs into SEC-regulated securities.

Today, Cohen is the founder and Executive Chairman of Reinvent Music Group (RMG), a progressive company forging a pioneering model for global digital distribution and collections that allows artists, composers and rightsholders to share in the financial value they generate.

Scott is also a committed human rights advocate. He serves on the Emeritus Board of the Fund for Global Human Rights and is a Founding Patron of In Place of War, the global arts charity that uses music, creativity and culture to drive positive change in communities affected by conflict.

Mandy Salem-Aubry

Managing Director, MSA Music Services · Leiden

30 years across publishing, rights management, sync and music-tech advisory.

Read bio of Mandy Salem-Aubry

Mandy Salem-Aubry is an independent music business professional with 30 years of international experience across multiple genres, sectors, markets, and cultures. Through MSA Music Services, she helps people and businesses navigate the music industry; her work spans publishing and rights management, business management, sync representation, music-tech advisory, partnership development and investor relations.

Her wider roster has included Slipknot, Jason Mraz, One Direction, Tori Amos, Nickelback, Moloko and Downtown Music Publishing. Before founding MSA Music Services, she held senior roles at Roadrunner Records, Fintage House and Songtrust.

Mandy is Conference Programme Associate for the IMPF Global Music Summit, a MiCannes committee member, and a regular moderator at international conferences. She is an Associate Member of the Independent Music Publishers Forum (IMPF) and a former Executive Board Member of the Association for Electronic Music (AFEM).
