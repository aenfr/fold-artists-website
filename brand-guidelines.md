# Fold Artists — Brand Identity System

## Brand Essence
**"Engineering the Compliance Layer for Gen-AI"**

Fold Artists sits at the intersection of institutional-grade fintech trust and bleeding-edge AI innovation. The brand communicates authority, precision, and transformative vision.

---

## Brand Story

### Mission
Build the essential AI/ML governance and compliance infrastructure for the generative era — solving the conflict between AI training data and copyrighted material.

### Vision
Unlock the €16B Gen-AI music economy by creating verifiable, transparent attribution at the moment of creation.

### Values
- **Precision** — Gradient-level accuracy, not approximation
- **Trust** — Institutional-grade infrastructure for rights holders
- **Innovation** — First-mover in causal attribution technology
- **Transparency** — Every royalty traceable, every influence quantified
- **Industry Leadership** — Backed by legends, built by engineers

---

## Logo

The Fold Artists mark is a geometric composition of folded planes in **Fold Orange** and **Fold Crimson**, forming a dynamic origami-like structure. It represents:
- The "fold" — transformation, layering, complexity made elegant
- Intersecting planes — the convergence of AI and IP
- Forward momentum — the angular cuts suggest progress

### Logo Usage
- Primary: Full-color on dark backgrounds (#0A0A0F)
- Monochrome: White version on dark backgrounds
- Minimum clear space: 1x logo height on all sides
- Never distort, rotate, or apply effects

## Brand Voice

### Tone Spectrum
```
Authoritative ←————●———→ Approachable
Technical     ←——●——————→ Simple
Confident     ←●————————→ Humble
Visionary     ←————●———→ Practical
```

### Voice Principles
1. **Speak with conviction** — We are building infrastructure, not pitching an idea
2. **Lead with impact** — Numbers, outcomes, transformation first
3. **Precision over hype** — "gradient-based attribution" not "revolutionary AI magic"
4. **Institutional tone** — Bloomberg, not TechCrunch
5. **Music industry fluency** — Speak the language of rights, royalties, catalogs

### Messaging Framework
- **Tagline:** "Engineering the Compliance Layer for Gen-AI"
- **Value Prop:** "The world's first gradient-based attribution engine"
- **Trust Signal:** "Patent Pending | WME Partner | Big Three Conversations"
- **CTA Language:** "Request Access" / "Schedule a Briefing" / "Join the Alliance"
